Frequently Asked Questions ❓

How do I place an order?
Click on any bouquet and fill in your details. You can order via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form. We'll confirm your order within 1 hour.
What is your delivery timeline?
We offer same-day delivery for orders placed before 2 PM. For deliveries outside the main area, please give us 24 hours notice.
Can I customize my bouquet?
Absolutely! We offer fully customizable arrangements. Just let us know your preferences, budget, and color scheme, and we'll create something special.
How long will the flowers last?
Our fresh flowers typically last 7-10 days with proper care. We include care instructions with every order to help extend their life.
